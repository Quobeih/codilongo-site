CodeIlonggo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026 · Applies to CodeIlonggo version 1.0 for Android

CodeIlonggo ("the app") is made by Nestor Jr Suerte. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

CodeIlonggo does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

What the app stores on your phone

To work, the app saves these on your device only:

  • your learning progress: completed lessons, quiz answers, solved problems, XP, levels, streaks, and achievements;
  • the code you write in the playground, so you can continue later;
  • your settings, such as language, theme, daily goal, and code font size.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings.

Running your code

Your code is compiled and run on your phone, inside a locked-down sandbox process that has no internet access and cannot read the app's other data. Your code is never uploaded.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in is needed, and the app does not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • No ads, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools, and no crash-reporting service.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or file access.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Read aloud

The "Read aloud" option uses your phone's own text-to-speech service to speak lesson text. The app sends that text only to the speech engine on your device. The speech engine is provided by your phone's maker or by Google, and its own privacy terms apply.

Children

CodeIlonggo is intended for learners aged 13 and older. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Future versions

A future update may add optional online features, such as accounts, cloud backup, or contests. If that happens, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and they will stay optional.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same link, with a new effective date.
